# Toolvantas — Project Status and Continuation Notes

## Project separation
Toolvantas is a separate project. Do not merge its code, branding, roadmap, or analytics with UtilityCanvas or AI Signal News.

## Approved homepage direction
**Hybrid: established utility-site usability with distinctive modern design.**
- Prioritize direct access to useful tools and categories.
- Use restrained typography, practical information density, and responsive layouts.
- Make the brand distinctive without copying a competitor's exact visual identity.
- Avoid fake usage statistics, testimonials, tool counts, or unsupported claims.
- The current homepage is a design iteration, not a claim that the full site is finished or deployed.

## First-tool research — preserve as approved
**Approved first tool:** Freelance Hourly & Day Rate Calculator.

**Primary keyword:** `freelance hourly rate calculator`

**Secondary keywords:**
- `hourly rate calculator for freelance`
- `freelance day rate calculator`
- `freelance rate calculator`

**User-provided third-party SBMM estimates (not independently verified ranking/revenue forecasts):**
- `freelance hourly rate calculator`: GSV 210/month, CPC $7.33, SBMM 35/100 (“Very easy win”), 5 weak sites/9 top 10, average DR 35.
- `freelance rate calculator`: GSV 880/month, CPC $16.05, SBMM 61/100 (“Moderate”), one weak site of 9, average DR 61.
- `freelance day rate calculator`: GSV 70/month, CPC $76.48, low ad competition, SBMM 34/100 (“Very easy win”), 3 weak sites/9, average DR 34.

These are third-party estimates, not guarantees of search rankings, traffic, or earnings.

**Approved feature intent:**
- Calculate a minimum sustainable hourly rate and day rate.
- Inputs: target income, business expenses, unpaid leave/non-billable days, and billable hours.
- Optional platform fees.
- Currency-agnostic; calculations run in the browser.
- Show a transparent formula and breakdown.
- Free and mobile-friendly.

**Current status:** Research and tool direction approved; functional calculator has not yet been implemented. The homepage labels it as in development and does not imply it is usable.

## Workflow
For each tool: Research → competitor/SERP analysis → finalize keyword and feature specification → build → test → fix → lock → record. Work one controlled step at a time. Do not claim completion without evidence.

## Deployment cautions

## Packaging adjustment (mobile upload)
To simplify GitHub upload from a phone, this package places `site.css` and `site.js` at the repository root rather than inside `assets/css/` and `assets/js/`. Both HTML files reference the root-level files. Upload the extracted files directly to the repository root; do not upload the ZIP archive itself.
