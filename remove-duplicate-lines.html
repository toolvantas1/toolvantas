How line deduplication works

Raw lists exported from spreadsheets, CRMs, server logs, or keyword research tools frequently contain hidden trailing spaces and mixed capitalization that cause naive deduplicators to miss duplicates:

  • Trim before matching: Enabling Trim leading/trailing spaces ensures "item" and "item " are recognized as identical.
  • Case-insensitive retention: When Case-sensitive is unchecked (recommended for emails, domains, and SEO keywords), Apple@example.com matches apple@example.com while preserving the capitalization of the first entry in your list.
  • Duplicate audit mode: Switch Output filter to Show only duplicated lines when you want to see which entries appeared two or more times rather than removing them silently.

Common data-cleaning workflows

  • Email & newsletter lists: Keep Case-sensitive off and Trim spaces on so subscribers never receive duplicate messages.
  • Linux paths, IDs & URLs with query tokens: Turn Case-sensitive on when uppercase and lowercase strings represent distinct file paths or base64 IDs.
  • Inventory SKUs & invoice numbers: Use Numeric (low → high) sorting to organize numbered identifiers naturally (so 2 comes before 10).

Frequently asked questions

Which copy of a duplicate line is kept?

The tool always keeps the very first occurrence it encounters from top to bottom (preserving its original capitalization when case-sensitive mode is off) and discards later repeats.

Why weren't two identical-looking lines merged?

Usually one line has invisible trailing spaces or tabs, or differs in letter casing. Make sure "Trim leading/trailing spaces" is checked and uncheck "Case-sensitive".

How can I find which items are duplicated in my list?

Change "Output filter" to "Show only duplicated lines". The output box will list each repeated item once so you can audit collisions quickly.

How large a list can I paste into this tool?

Because deduplication uses a linear-time JavaScript Map/Set in your browser, lists of 50,000+ lines process in a fraction of a second on modern devices.

How does Numeric sort handle text mixed with numbers?

It uses locale-aware natural collation (numeric: true), ordering Item 2 before Item 10 instead of sorting character-by-character.

Are my email lists or log lines sent to a server?

No. All processing happens locally inside your browser tab; your data is never transmitted or saved externally.
