    Why client-side PDF merging protects your privacy

    Most commercial online PDF mergers upload your documents to third-party cloud servers where they are queued, processed, and temporarily stored. For sensitive contracts, medical records, or tax paperwork, remote uploads create privacy liabilities.

    • No Server Uploads: This tool utilizes the client-side pdf-lib WebAssembly/JS engine. Document binary bytes are processed exclusively within your device's local memory.
    • Total Compliance: Because no data ever leaves your browser, your files remain fully compliant with strict corporate and privacy policies.
    • Zero Wait Queues: Without network bottlenecks or server queues, merging takes place at the speed of your device hardware.

    Best practices for merging PDF documents

    • Arrange the order first: Use the ▲ and ▼ buttons to ensure cover pages, tables of contents, and appendices sit in the proper sequence before merging.
    • Unlock protected files: Ensure your source PDF documents are not password-protected before merging, as encrypted files cannot be read without authentication.
    • Retain your source files: Merging creates a separate, new PDF document without altering or deleting your original source files.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my confidential PDF files uploaded to any server?

    No. None of your PDF documents are ever uploaded to any external server. All merging, page extraction, and compilation happens in-memory directly on your device.

    Is there a limit on how many PDF files I can merge?

    There is no arbitrary software limit. You can merge as many documents as your computer or smartphone's available memory can accommodate.

    Can I change the order of the files before merging?

    Yes. Simply click the up (▲) or down (▼) arrow buttons next to any document in the list to rearrange the merge sequence.

    What happens if one of my PDFs is password-protected?

    Password-protected or encrypted PDFs cannot be opened by the browser engine for security reasons. You must decrypt the document before combining it with other files.
