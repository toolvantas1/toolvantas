Why use a client-side batch PDF page counter?

Counting pages across numerous documents is a daily task in legal discovery, medical record indexing, printing shops, and transcription work. Relying on cloud services creates unnecessary risk.

  • 100% In-Browser Privacy: Your confidential litigation files, financial filings, and personal tax returns are read directly in browser memory using pdf-lib. No documents are ever uploaded to any server.
  • Unlimited Batch Capacity: Drag dozens of files at once without waiting for server queues or hitting arbitrary daily limits.
  • Orientation Intelligence: Identifies which pages are portrait versus landscape, which is essential for printing setups and bindery calculations.

Estimating printing, scanning, and translation costs

  • Commercial Print Jobs: Enter your print shop's per-page cost to calculate client quotes instantly for black-and-white or color copy orders.
  • Scanning & Digitization: Estimate processing costs for high-volume document scanning projects based on total page counts.
  • Document Indexing: Paralegals and litigation support teams can generate itemized CSV logs to audit document discovery productions.
  • Instant CSV Logs: Download a formatted spreadsheet ready to open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to any server when counting pages?

No. All parsing and page inspections run 100% in-memory on your device using client-side JavaScript. None of your files or metadata are ever transmitted to any external server.

Can I upload dozens of PDF files at once?

Yes. Drag and drop multiple PDF files simultaneously and the counter inspects each file asynchronously in local browser memory.

How does the print and billing cost estimator work?

Select your currency and enter your rate per page (for example, 0.08 for 8 cents per page). The calculator multiplies this rate by the total valid page count across all files.

Can I export an itemized breakdown of all file page counts?

Yes. Click "Export CSV Report" to download a clean spreadsheet containing file names, file sizes, page counts, and portrait/landscape sheet counts.

Does this tool detect landscape vs. portrait pages?

Yes. It reads the native width, height, and rotation angle of every page, reporting exact counts for portrait versus landscape sheets.
