How to create an invoice that gets paid fast

An effective invoice eliminates ambiguity for accounting departments and clients. Following standard billing practices speeds up payouts and prevents disputes.

  • Clear invoice numbering: Use a sequential or year-based numbering pattern (e.g. INV-2026-001) so accounts payable can track the bill.
  • Precise line items: Detail the specific scope, milestones, or hours delivered rather than a generic single-line description.
  • Visible payment terms: State explicit due dates and accepted payment methods (ACH, wire details, or payment portal instructions).
  • Tax IDs: If registered for sales tax, VAT, or GST, list your registration number clearly under your business address.

Print & PDF Exporting Instructions

  • Click the Print / Save as PDF button at the bottom of the editor.
  • In the browser print dialog that appears, set the destination printer to Save as PDF.
  • Under More settings, set Margins to Default or Minimum, and make sure Background graphics is checked so table headers render cleanly.
  • The print stylesheet suppresses all web controls, navigation bars, related links, and footers, creating a crisp professional invoice document.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator completely free?

Yes, 100% free with no watermarks, limitations, or account sign-ups. Your data stays entirely in your web browser.

How do I download the invoice as a PDF file?

Click the "Print / Save as PDF" button. In your browser's print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination printer. Your invoice will be downloaded directly to your device.

Is my client or financial data saved on any server?

No. All calculations and previews run completely inside your local browser. No financial data or contact details are sent to any remote server.

Can I bill in multiple currencies?

Yes. You can switch between USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, BDT, INR, and PKR from the currency dropdown.

How are discounts and taxes applied on the invoice?

The percentage discount is subtracted from the line-item subtotal first, and the tax or VAT percentage is calculated on the discounted subtotal.
