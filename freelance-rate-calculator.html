How the calculator works

Your rate needs to cover your income goal, business expenses, the tax reserve you choose, and platform fees. It must also account for time spent on admin, marketing and other non-billable work.

  • Annual billable hours: working hours per week × (52 − weeks off) × billable share.
  • Income after tax reserve: target income ÷ (1 − estimated tax rate).
  • Costs: annual expenses × (1 + overhead markup).
  • Revenue before fees: tax-adjusted income + costs.
  • Revenue needed: revenue before fees ÷ ((1 − platform fee) × (1 − payout/conversion fee)).
  • Minimum hourly rate: revenue needed ÷ annual billable hours.
  • Recommended rate: minimum rate × (1 + optional buffer).
  • Day rate: recommended hourly rate × billable hours per day.

Important: This is a planning estimate, not tax or financial advice. The tax reserve is a simplified estimate, not a jurisdiction-specific tax calculation. Check your actual tax and payment fees. Market rates vary by skills, experience, client, location and project scope.
