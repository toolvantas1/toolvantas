    How speed & velocity conversions work

    Speed measures distance traveled per unit of time. Every unit in this converter is anchored to the SI derived unit, meters per second (m/s), using exact international definitions:

    • Kilometers per hour (km/h): 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h exactly.
    • Miles per hour (mph): 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s = 1.609344 km/h exactly.
    • Knots (kn): One international nautical mile per hour—1 kn = 1.852 km/h ≈ 1.15078 mph.
    • Feet per second (ft/s): Used in physics, ballistics, and wind engineering—1 ft/s = 0.3048 m/s.
    • Mach number: Ratio of speed to the local speed of sound (340.29 m/s or 1,225.04 km/h at ISA sea level, 15 °C).

    Practical uses for speed & pace

    • Driving & road limits: 100 km/h is ~62.14 mph; 60 mph is ~96.56 km/h; 70 mph is ~112.65 km/h.
    • Weather & wind reports: Meteorologists report wind in m/s, knots, or km/h. A tropical storm begins at 34 knots (~63 km/h or ~39 mph), and a Category 1 hurricane begins at 64 knots (~119 km/h or ~74 mph).
    • Running & treadmill training: Treadmills show speed in mph or km/h, whereas race plans use pace in minutes per kilometer or mile. A treadmill set to 12 km/h equals a 5:00 /km (8:03 /mi) pace.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert km/h to mph?

    Divide kilometers per hour by 1.609344 (or multiply by 0.621371). For example, 100 km/h × 0.621371 = 62.14 mph.

    How do I convert m/s to km/h?

    Multiply meters per second by 3.6. Because 1 hour has 3,600 seconds and 1 kilometer has 1,000 meters, 10 m/s equals exactly 36 km/h.

    What is a knot and why do ships and aircraft use it?

    One knot equals 1 nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h or 1.15078 mph). Navigators prefer knots because 1 nautical mile corresponds directly to 1 minute of latitude on charts.

    How is running pace (min/km or min/mi) calculated from speed?

    Pace is the inverse of speed: divide 60 minutes by your speed in km/h (or mph). At 10 km/h, 60 ÷ 10 = 6:00 minutes per kilometer.

    Does Mach 1 always equal the same speed in km/h?

    No. Mach 1 depends on air temperature. At sea level (15 °C), Mach 1 is about 1,225 km/h (761 mph), while at commercial cruising altitude (~11,000 m, −56.5 °C) it drops to about 1,062 km/h (660 mph).

    How fast is the speed of light?

    In a vacuum, light travels at exactly 299,792,458 meters per second—about 1.079 billion km/h or 670.6 million mph.
