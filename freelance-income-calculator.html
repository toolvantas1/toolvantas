How freelance take-home income is estimated

Start with average monthly earnings. Subtract platform commissions, payment or withdrawal costs, fixed payout charges, and business expenses. Then reserve a percentage of positive profit for estimated taxes.

Take-home = gross income − percentage fees − fixed fees − expenses − tax reserve.

Annual figures multiply the monthly estimate by 12. Compare low, typical, and high scenarios if your income varies.

What should you include?

  • Gross income: client billings before deductions.
  • Platform fees: marketplace commissions (e.g., Upwork, Fiverr).
  • Payment costs: card processing, withdrawal, or conversion fees.
  • Business expenses: software, internet, equipment, and recurring work costs.
  • Tax reserve: a planning percentage chosen for your jurisdiction.

Frequently asked questions

Is this my exact after-tax income?

No. It is an estimate, not a tax return or statutory tax calculation. Rules vary by country and individual circumstances; consult a qualified tax professional.

Does it support different currencies?

Yes. Choose a display currency. It does not convert currencies or fetch exchange rates, so enter all amounts in the selected currency.

What if my freelance income changes every month?

Change gross monthly income to compare low, typical, and high scenarios. Annual figures are monthly estimates multiplied by 12.

Can I use this for direct client work without marketplace fees?

Yes. Set platform fees to zero when no platform commission applies.

How is the tax reserve applied when expenses exceed income?

The tax reserve percentage applies only to positive pre-tax profit remaining after fees and business expenses. If pre-tax profit is zero or negative, the tax reserve is 0.
