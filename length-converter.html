Metric vs. Imperial length systems

Length and distance are measured using two primary international measurement frameworks:

  • The International Metric System (SI): Based on decimals and powers of 10 with the standard meter as its foundation. Kilometers ($10^3$), centimeters ($10^{-2}$), and millimeters ($10^{-3}$) simplify scale adjustments without complex fractions.
  • US Customary & Imperial System: Uses historical units including inches, feet, yards, and miles. A foot contains 12 inches, a yard contains 3 feet (36 inches), and a statute mile contains 5,280 feet.
  • Nautical Distance: One nautical mile ($1,852\text{ meters}$) corresponds to one minute of latitude on Earth's surface, forming the global standard for maritime navigation and aviation.

Frequently asked questions

How many inches are in a centimeter, and how many centimeters are in an inch?

By international treaty (1959), exactly 1 inch equals 2.54 centimeters. Conversely, 1 centimeter is approximately equal to 0.393701 inches.

How do I convert feet and inches (e.g. 5'10") to centimeters?

Multiply the number of feet by 12 and add the remaining inches to calculate total inches, then multiply by 2.54. For example, 5 feet 10 inches equals 70 total inches, which multiplied by 2.54 gives exactly 177.8 centimeters.

What is the difference between a statute mile and a nautical mile?

A statute mile (standard land mile) measures 5,280 feet or approximately 1,609.344 meters. A nautical mile is slightly longer at 1,852 meters (approx. 6,076.12 feet), designed around navigation along Earth's coordinates.

What base unit does this calculator use for internal conversion?

All calculations normalize through the international standard meter (m) before scaling to target units, guaranteeing zero cumulative rounding drift across conversions.

Can this tool handle microscopic measurements like micrometers and nanometers?

Yes. Micrometers (microns, $10^{-6}\text{ m}$) and nanometers ($10^{-9}\text{ m}$) are natively supported with high-precision decimal formatting.
